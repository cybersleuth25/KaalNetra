# KaalNetra — Easy Guide & Visual Walkthrough

> **What is KaalNetra?**  
> An interactive **history strategy game** where you step inside the famous **Siege of Chittorgarh (1567–1568 CE)**.  
> You make critical defense choices, watch how your decisions affect the fort, and then compare what happened in your game against real history.

## 2. Who Are You and What Is Your Goal?

- **The Setting**: It is October 1567. Mughal Emperor Akbar marches on Chittorgarh with 60,000 troops and heavy cannons.
- **Your Job**: You advise the Rajput garrison leaders: **Rao Jaimal** (careful wall commander) and **Rawat Patta** (fearless counter-attacker).
- **The Catch**: 
  1. The fort sits on a 500-foot tall cliff — almost impossible to climb.
  2. But there is **no outside army coming to rescue you**.
  3. You only have the **food and rainwater stored inside the fort**.
  4. 30,000 ordinary villagers are sheltering with you.

## 4. The 2 Main Decisions

### Decision 1: Enemy Diggers Approaching (December 1567)
Akbar's troops build giant covered wooden tunnels (*sabats*) to drag cannons up the cliff.
- **Option A (Guard Walls)**: Stay inside. Saves soldiers, but allows enemy cannons to get closer.
- **Option B (Ration Food)**: Strict food conservation. Saves grain, but lowers soldier energy.
- **Option C (Night Raid)**: Send a strike team out to burn enemy tunnels. Slows enemy, but you lose brave men.

### Decision 2: The Gunpowder Explosion (January 1568)
Enemy diggers place gunpowder barrels under the wall — **BOOM!** A 40-foot breach opens!
- **Option A (Rush to the Breach)**: Send elite soldiers to plug the hole. Saves the fort, but causes heavy casualties.
- **Option B (Build Inner Wall)**: Fall back and build a barricade under archer cover. Balanced defense.
- **Option C (Night Strike on Sappers)**: Attack the enemy tunnel entrance directly. Risky and costly.

## 6. Fast 3-Minute Playthrough Guide

1. **Home Screen**: Click **"Enter the Archives"**.
2. **Scenario Select**: Click **"Examine Situation"** under *Chittorgarh 1567*.
3. **Story Context**: Read the 4 quick points about the attack, then click **"Meet Defense Team"**.
4. **Defense Briefing**: See your 2 commanders and fort resources, then click **"Begin Defense"**.
5. **Decision 1**: Choose an option (e.g., *Option C: Night Raid*), click **"Confirm Decision"**.
6. **Simulation 1**: See the bars change! (Soldiers -9, Enemy Danger -5, Morale +7). Click **"Proceed to Next Council"**.
7. **Decision 2**: Choose how to handle the blown-up wall (e.g., *Option A: Rush the Breach*).
8. **Final Outcome**: See your defense rating (**Strong Defense**, **Barely Held On**, etc.).
9. **History Compare**: Click **"See Real History Comparison"** to see what Akbar and Jaimal actually did in 1568!
10. **Review**: Answer 4 fun questions about trade-offs, then click **"Play Again"** to try a different choice!
